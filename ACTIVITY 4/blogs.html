Math Topics

Math helps us understand patterns, solve problems, and describe the world around us. Explore some of its essential topics below.

Arithmetic

Work with numbers using addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, and percentages.

Algebra

Use variables, expressions, and equations to represent relationships and find unknown values.

Geometry

Study shapes, angles, lines, area, and volume to understand space and measurement.

Statistics and probability

Collect and interpret data, describe patterns, and reason about how likely events are.

Trigonometry

Explore the relationships between angles and sides of triangles, useful in science and engineering.

Calculus

Use limits, derivatives, and integrals to study change and accumulation.
